# Decisions

One line per meaningful choice, with the reason.

- **Stack: React 19 + TS + Vite + vite-plugin-pwa, no PixiJS/Zustand/TanStack/Dexie/Supabase.** Static GitHub Pages hosting; Canvas 2D is plenty for 32×32 sprites; a 40-line store with useSyncExternalStore + idb-keyval keeps the shell at ~137 KB gz.
- **No backend.** GitHub Pages is static; accounts, sync, RLS, push notifications and community challenges are out. ONB-8 "Save your journal?" became a backup-file prompt; the offline "N waiting to sync" chip became "saved on this phone".
- **Palette slot mapping by nearest default colour, not luminance sort (PIX-5).** Luminance sort paints robins grey; pulling each species slot toward its nearest photo cluster plus a global light shift keeps anatomy readable while the photo still decides the colours.
- **Sprites stored as 17 palette indices, frames regenerated deterministically.** Tiny storage, byte-identical on every device; `spriteHash` of frame 0 is kept for audits.
- **Ink reserved for outlines; big black areas render as Plum.** Black birds otherwise vanish on dark pages.
- **Window Mode species are filed as verified without a confirm sheet.** The game spawned the bird, so it is not a silent guess; keeps first catch at 6 taps.
- **Window Mode palette comes from a clean plate (bird + scene average colour), the photo is the real scene crop.** Painted backgrounds would otherwise bleed into sprite colours.
- **Craft badges count Field Mode photos only.** Window-mode Fieldcraft is partly synthetic (focus/patience), so it can't farm photo badges.
- **"Not sure — identify later" instead of "you tell me".** There is no ID model; the honest copy says what happens.
- **Hotspot/county badges re-expressed as patch/habitat badges.** No coordinates are stored, so hotspots and counties cannot be known.
- **Call player links to Xeno-canto instead of embedding audio.** Recordings need per-file licences and an API key.
- **Reference photos from iNaturalist (user request).** Only CC-licensed default photos, always attributed; cached by the service worker for offline.
- **Owner-supplied layered backgrounds replace procedural scenes (user request).** 14 sets mapped to habitat × day phase; procedural pixel props (branches, wire, feeder, post) give birds perches in every scene. The art is outside FIELD 32 by owner choice; sprites and props stay in FIELD 32.
- **UI theme: near-black neutrals with a neon yellow-green primary (#C6FF1A) (user request).** Sprite palette unchanged; "gold" badge tier and Legendary rarity keep FIELD 32 Sun.
- **Mobile-only layout.** The app is a 480 px column; desktop shows a note to open it on a phone.
- **Sun times from region centroid (NOAA approx.) placed relative to solar noon.** Fixes sunsets that wrap past UTC midnight; tested across longitudes.
- **No decorations (user request).** Feathers are spent on feeder food only; extra Aviary scenes open at 10 species.
- **One job per tab (user request).** Explore = find birds (full-screen map + pull-up drawer), BirdDex = collection, Snap = camera, Aviary = care (Birds · Feeder), Trainer = progress (Quests · Badges · Lenses · Bird list). Quests live only in Trainer; alerts surface where action is needed.
- **Discovery language (user request).** Birds are found and discovered, never caught: "Discovered!", the Snap Lens records a bird's likeness while the real bird flies on.
- **Owner-supplied sound clips** for purchases, new BirdDex entries, level-ups/badges and the Snap Lens throw; long jingles duck the music.
- **Professor Merlin (owner art) is the only guide.** Merlin (a real small falcon) leads onboarding, runs the Feed Store and the quest board. Rowan the druid was removed at the owner's request.
- **Bird Homes unlock by trainer level.** 51 owner-supplied scenes (Glacier lake removed); 4 at Lv 1, then 2 per level to Lv 25. Trainer level = 1 + floor(sqrt(XP/30)), finer than the 9 ranks so there is always an unlock in reach.
- **Map tiles: OpenStreetMap.** CARTO basemaps began requiring an API key; OSM standard tiles need none (with OSM France as a fallback).
- **Music only on the title and onboarding.** In-game music is opt-in (Settings → Music during play).
- **Main theme is a streamed song.** Kept out of the install precache (2 MB) and cached on first play; it pauses/resumes across screens and ducks under jingles.
- **Plain-language bird groups.** Songbird, Crow family, Bird of prey… replace invented type names; BirdDex filters are All/Found/Missing + group + where.
- **Live data needs no login.** iNaturalist read endpoints are public with CORS `*`; Explore shows a live status, refreshes every 5 minutes, lists the latest nearby reports, and Settings has a live-data check.
- **Sound only while the app is on screen.** All audio pauses on visibilitychange/pagehide and resumes on return.

- **Snap Lenses boost Snap Power.** Five lenses (Ripple free +0; Bubble +5, Ember +10, Arcane +15, Phantom +20, bought with Feathers) add to the photo score of camera shots (capped at 100), which raises XP. Each also tints the capsule and plays an owner-supplied fx sheet when a bird is recorded, replacing the old explosion. Viewable and switchable on the Trainer page and in Merlin's Shop.
- **Merlin's guided tour** runs once after onboarding (and on first launch of this version for existing players). It spotlights the real UI on each tab; skippable, keyboard-operable (Esc, arrows) and replayable from Settings.
- **Snap Power is explained where it appears** (onboarding, the result dialog, and the label on the info box): it is the photo score out of 100 and only affects XP.
- **Colour themes:** Neon (default), Dusk, Ember and Field guide (light). Each passes axe-core colour-contrast on every main screen (WCAG 2.1 AA, Section 508).
- **Identification is a skill moment.** Picking a candidate opens a side-by-side compare (your photo vs a CC reference photo + short description) before confirming; "Try next" flips through the shortlist.
- **First win, fast.** The first discovery pays double XP and Feathers. "It flew off? Log it without a photo" records a seen-only sighting (4 Feathers, 0.25× XP) that marks the BirdDex cell as seen but never counts as discovered or joins the Aviary.
- **BirdDex chapters** (Backyard 12, Park & Woods 20, Water 15, Hunters 10, and a monthly "specials" set from iNaturalist month counts) give finishable goals with Feather rewards; the whole-region total is secondary.
- **One progression number.** Level (1 + √(XP/30)) is primary; rank names are titles. Snap Power shows as a grade (S ≥90, A ≥75, B ≥55, C ≥35, D).
- **Just-in-time help.** Merlin shows a one-time tip on each tab and in the camera; the full 12-step tour only runs from Settings → Replay.
- **Shop everywhere.** The Feather counter opens Merlin's Shop as its own screen.
- **Missions** turn the freshest nearby sighting of a bird you still need into a 3-hour goal (+25 XP, +25 Feathers), with walking directions via Google Maps.
- **Pets you can touch; hunger that forgives.** Tapping a bird makes it hop and chirp and shows its HP and mood; each bird gives 3 Feathers once a day. After 20+ hours away, Merlin feeds weak Aviary birds back to 50 HP and says so.
- **Settings diet.** Grid, reference photos, live-data check, sprite lab and diagnostics live under Advanced. Diagnostics shows a local player-journey summary (no data leaves the phone).
- **BirdDex is a field guide.** Goals (chapters) → look-up search → All / Found / To find → a sticky group index → one section per bird group with a found count. Unfound birds show their name (a guide you can look things up in), as a silhouette. "Life list" is now "Bird list".
- **Equal heights on every row.** BirdDex cards are a fixed 4-row grid (number / art / name / footer); chapter cards, pantry tiles, button pairs, Explore cards, Bird Home and theme cards stretch to the tallest item in their row.
- **Pixel food icons** replace emoji so Seed Mix, Berry Mash and Mealworm Tub match the art style on every phone.
- **Missions can be declined.** "Not now" hides mission offers for 2 hours, and that species is not offered again that day.
- **Bird Homes live on the Aviary page** as a horizontal strip (no extra "Change" step); the chosen home is centred, locked homes show the level they unlock at.
- **Sheets render at the app root** (React portal) so they always sit at the bottom of the screen, even when the page behind them is scrolled.
- **Fun facts are sourced, never generated.** Tapping a bird shows the curated BirdlyQuest fact for its species, then whole sentences from its Wikipedia summary (via iNaturalist), one per tap.
- **The partner is a pet too.** It has HP, gets hungry, eats from the feeder and is cared for by Merlin's welcome-back feeding, so its HP bar shows above it like every other bird.
- **Birds can be named.** Right after a discovery (optional field) or any time from the Aviary list (✎ Name). Names show in the Aviary, its labels, the Bird Box and meal messages; the species name stays alongside.
- **35 Snap Lenses in four tiers** (Common Lv1, Rare Lv4, Epic Lv8, Legendary Lv12; +0 to +30 Snap Power, 0–600 Feathers). Effects reuse the five owner fx sheets with CSS colour filters and eight layouts (single, double, ring of 3, ring of 6, swarm, rain, orbit, rainbow).
- **Feathers read as money.** A gold pixel feather marks every balance and price; the counter says "Feathers"; Merlin's Shop opens with your balance, "Feathers are BirdlyQuest's money" and a How-to-earn list; Merlin explains it in onboarding.
- **Bug sweep:** a scripted run through ~55 screens and states in all four themes checks JS errors, failed requests, broken images, sideways overflow and axe a11y rules (contrast, names, labels). Fixes from it: Escape closes the badge pop-up, tier colours are theme-aware, page subtitles wrap instead of truncating.
- **Lens bodies differ by tier** (Common grey capsule, Rare steel body with silver handles, Epic purple winged body with a gem, Legendary gold crowned body with rubies and a glow), and every lens above the starter adds a pixel-particle burst in its own shape and colours (drops, leaves, hearts, snowflakes, stars, bolts, diamonds, sparkles, rings, feathers).
- **Default Bird Home is day/night:** Sky meadow by day, Mountain dusk at dusk and night. New homes from the owner: Sky meadow (Lv1), Crimson castle (Lv9), Moonlit keep (Lv13), Ivy chapel (Lv17). The strip is ordered by unlock level.
- **Birding spots on Explore:** iNaturalist community places near you (parks, refuges, preserves, wetlands…), filtered to nature places, ranked by research-grade bird species recorded there, with map pins, "often seen" birds and walking directions. Cached for a day per area; no new services or keys.
- **Explore drawer has one size.** The All / Less toggle (and the expanding handle) was removed; each tab scrolls inside the drawer.
- **Camera fills the screen.** The live view uses the full height; Likely birds (one compact row), zoom / shutter / Import and "It flew off?" float on a gradient over the bottom. A square frame shows exactly what is captured (the crop is mapped from the on-screen frame back to video pixels, including software zoom). Merlin's camera tip sits at the top.
- **Sky meadow trimmed** to its scenery band so the mountains and clouds sit in the Aviary view instead of being cropped off.
- **New players start with two Bird Homes** (Sky meadow and Mountain dusk, the default day/night pair); every other home unlocks one level later than before (Lv2–Lv26). Camera Likely cards are larger (photo, name, sprite) and the camera shutter is 64px.
- **Page headers:** title and actions (Feathers, settings) share one line; the subtitle runs full width underneath so it does not wrap.
- **In-season birds are cards** on the Likely tab (up to 8, same card as Likely), above the Likely list.
- **Explore cards never clip names:** cards are 112px wide with room for three lines; names wrap in full and every card in a row stretches to the same height.
- **Camera zoom and brightness work the same way.** Matching vertical rails, with zoom on the left and brightness on the right. Drag up for more and drag down for less. Double-tap a rail to reset it, and arrow keys work too. On the live view itself, a one-finger drag up or down zooms, a pinch zooms, and a tap still focuses. Zoom is continuous (log-scaled): it uses the camera's own zoom where the phone exposes one (capped at 10×) and software zoom up to 4× where it doesn't. A large readout shows the value while you change it. Hardware updates are coalesced so a drag never queues dozens of `applyConstraints` calls.
- **Likely birds are tappable in the camera.** Tapping one slides a bird card over the bottom of the screen: reference photo, rarity, whether you've found it, size, groups and a fact. Swipe or use ‹ › to move between the five Likely birds. The camera keeps running above the card, so getting back is instant: the big "Back to camera" button, a tap on the live view, a swipe down, Esc, the top-bar arrow, or the phone's Back gesture. A small back-guard in `nav.tsx` makes Back close the card instead of the whole camera. "Full field guide page" opens the species page on top, and Back returns to the card with the camera still live.
- **The camera bird card always shows the real bird.** Tapping a Likely bird is an explicit request, so the card fetches the CC-licensed iNaturalist photo even when passive reference photos are switched off in Settings. Photos for all five Likely birds are fetched and decoded when the camera opens. The small square (usually cached already) shows at once and the full-size photo fades in over it. Live species fall back to their own iNaturalist photo. If there is truly no photo, or the phone is offline, the card says so and offers "Try again" (offline retries automatically on reconnect). It never substitutes a pixel sprite for the real bird.
- **Brightness is a button; the zoom gauge stays (v1.16, user request).** The brightness slider was removed. The button where the zoom button used to be (left of the shutter) steps brightness 0 → +1 → +2 → −1 → 0 and lights up when brightness isn't 0. Zoom stays on the left gauge, by dragging or pinching on the live view. The bottom controls were slimmed down (smaller Likely cards and shutter, tighter spacing), from about 190px to about 150px, to give the camera view more room.
- **Import left, brightness right; the zoom gauge hides until used (v1.17, user request).** The gauge is invisible (and not touchable) until you touch the live view or zoom. It stays while your finger is on it and fades out about 1.6s after the last touch. Keyboard focus also reveals it. Drag-to-zoom and pinch on the view work whether or not it's showing.
- **BirdDex goals are a quest board; the currency is "Feather Coins" (v1.18, user request).** Each goal card has its own colour and pixel emblem, a "N to go" line, and a row of the goal's birds (found ones in colour, missing ones as silhouettes). It also has a segmented progress bar with three star milestones (a third, two thirds, all) and a treasure chest. The chest is locked while you're collecting, glows and bobs once the goal is complete, and bursts Feather Coins when opened. Ribbons mark "Next goal", "Complete!" and "Mastered". Rewards and claiming are unchanged. Every player-facing "Feathers" now reads "Feather Coins". Code names (`feathers`, `FeatherChip`) and real-bird feather descriptions are unchanged.
- **Field Guide headings are typewriter, not pixel (v1.19, user request).** In the Field Guide theme, headings (screen titles, section titles, goal names, bird-card names) use Courier semi-bold (600) with letter-spacing −0.05em, so Courier's wide monospace letters sit closer together. It matches the theme's Courier body text. Other themes keep the Silkscreen pixel headings. The camera's zoom hint also moved up into the top ~28% of the view.
- **Field Trials: friendly, bird-true contests (v1.20, user request).** Reached from the Aviary. Rounds are what real birds compete at: **Song-off** (song variety), **Display** (plumage and courtship), **Sky race** (speed and wingspan) and **Forage** (clever food-finding). Stats come from biology: an archetype base, adjusted by family (mockingbirds and wrens sing, cardinals and wood ducks dazzle, falcons and swallows fly, crows and chickadees forage), by wingspan for Sky, and by a few famous species. Training (the bird's level and photo quality) adds at most about 28, so biology still decides most rounds. Each round adds a ±8 roll. A margin of 25 or more is a "Critical!". You see the rival's three contests up front and choose which bird enters each one (each bird once; with fewer than three birds, your best may repeat). Your partner bird can compete, so new players can try it straight away. The ladder has 6 rivals (Pip → Wren → Captain Gale → Mara → Old Hawkins → Professor Merlin), with rival birds drawn from your region. The first win pays the full prize (25–120 Feather Coins plus XP). Rematches pay 5 coins and 15 XP, three times a day. Losing costs nothing, and birds never lose health. Assets come from the owner's pack. Backgrounds reuse the nature scenes already in the app (pine forest, sunrise tree, mountain dusk, valley pond). Effects are a sound wave and ring (Song), a glitter orb and star burst (Display), a wind gust (Sky), leaves (Forage), a fiery blast (Critical) and a nova ring (round win). There are 11 sounds. The pack's character sprites were not used because they all carry weapons.
- **Field Trials v2: Pokémon-style turn-based battles (v1.21, user request).** This replaces the watch-only lineup show. Each turn you choose **Perform** (four moves), **Bag** (pantry food: Seed Mix +20 Spirit, Berry Mash +½ Spirit, Mealworm Tub flair +2; uses up the bag), **Birds** (switch; costs your turn) or **Run** (no penalty). **Spirit** replaces HP: being outshone drains it, and at 0 the bird "flies off to rest", so it's never framed as damage. Each bird's **type** is its best style. The type chart is a loop (Song > Display > Sky > Forage > Song) with ×2 "super impressive" and ×½ "not very impressive" messages. There's a 1.25× same-style bonus, a 1/16 "show-stopping" critical, accuracy misses, and limited uses per move. Moves come from the bird's style ladder (stronger at Lv10 and Lv20), plus one move in its second style and a signature move: Alarm Call (lowers rival flair), Courtship Dance (+2 flair), Updraft (+1 composure) or Preen (heal). Damage uses a Pokémon-like formula. Attack is natural ability plus photo quality ÷ 8. The rival AI picks the best expected move, with some randomness, heals when low, and sometimes opens with a boost. Parties are up to 3 birds; rivals bring 1–3. Rival levels track your party (`max(base, avg × (0.78 + tier × 0.05))`). Simulated win rates for a basic player at Lv12: Pip 100%, Wren 98%, Gale 95%, Mara 85%, Hawkins 3%, Merlin 0%. At Lv18, Hawkins 43% and Merlin 17%. That's a deliberate gym-style progression, and it's easier with switching, items and status moves. Winning while the prize still pays levels up every bird that took part (+1, max 50). The layout follows the classic battle screen: rival top-right on a platform, your bird large at bottom-left, cream info boxes with Lv, type, party balls and a Spirit bar, a typewriter text box (tap to advance), and a 2×2 command menu (red, yellow, green, blue), plus a VS intro splash. Each rival has a home backdrop (valley, pine, palm shore, sunrise tree, mountain dusk, misty wood).
- **Field Trials v3: Step Quest-style battles with a modern HUD (v1.22, user request).** The v2 Pokémon-style menus were confusing and slow, so this replaces them with one tap per turn and every button explaining itself:
  - **Quick move:** always ready. It's named for the bird's style (Chirp, Show Off, Swoop or Peck).
  - **Signature skill:** a real talent with a one-line effect and the fact behind it. It recharges for a turn. Examples: a hawk's Stoop Dive (often critical), a chickadee's Seed Cache (heals), a woodpecker's Drum Roll (3 hits), an owl's Silent Flight (goes through Guard), a duck's Waterproof (heals and shelters), a heron's Patient Stalk (next move ×2.2). 15 skills, mapped by family, then archetype.
  - **Guard:** blocks 60% of the next hit.
  - **Snack:** pantry food. Seed Mix +30% HP, Berry Mash +60%, Mealworms +40% power for 3 turns.
  - **Super:** a gauge that fills as you act and as you're hit; a ×3 hit that can't miss or be guarded.
  - **Run away.**
  - **The rival:** quick move, its own signature skill, a guard when low, and every 4th turn a **Big Move it announces a turn ahead** (red "Guard!" banner).
  - **Pace:** HP replaces Spirit. When a bird rests, the next one flies in automatically (no manual switching). No type chart. A turn timer applies after your first battles (12s for Pip, down to 7s for Merlin; timing out passes your turn). 25% of timed battles open with an ambush.
  - **Balance (simulated):** smart play (skill when ready, guard Big Moves, Super when full) beats quick-move spam by a wide margin. Merlin at Lv12: 76% vs 3%. A 3-vs-3 battle takes about 9 taps.
  - **First battle:** a guided 7-step tutorial with Merlin that spotlights each button and waits for you to use it (HUD → quick → skill, with its real fact → Guard against a scripted Big Move → Snack → Super, gauge filled → timer). There's no timer, and the rival has ×2.6 HP so the lessons fit.
  - **Challenges:** after you log a new species (from your 2nd bird on), there's a 40% chance a wandering birder challenges you, at most once a day. The new bird leads. The win prize is 15 Feather Coins, 30 XP and +1 level for the birds that took part. The prompt waits until badge pop-ups are dismissed.
  - **Battle music:** plays `public/music/battle.mp3` when present (with Music on), otherwise the built-in chiptune battle theme. The previous music is restored after.
  - **Layout:** glass HUD capsules (name, Lv, HP bar with trailing damage, rolling numbers, party pips), a ring timer, floating damage numbers, a glowing Super bar, 2×2 action cards and a "Battle mode" title card. Bird and platform now share one anchor, so rival birds stand on their platforms instead of floating. All labels are single-line with ellipsis. Scanned at 320px and 390px in both themes with the real pixel font, with no overflow.
- **Battle mode in the app's own style, with real battle music (v1.23, user request).** The Field Trials screens now use the app's components and theme tokens instead of a separate look:
  - **Components:** `.btn` (square, 2px black border, hard shadow), `.panel`, the cream `.dialog` box for battle messages and Merlin's tutorial, square HUD panels, segmented meters, and steps()-based retro animations.
  - **Fonts:** labels use `--lbl`, which is Silkscreen, or Courier semi-bold in the Field Guide theme. Colours follow the active theme.
  - **HP panels:** a `minmax(0, 1fr) auto minmax(0, 1fr)` grid so both always fit. Under 360px wide they get tighter padding and smaller names, and the "HP" suffix is hidden. Under 700px and 600px tall, the birds scale down and the tray compacts.
  - **Verified:** at 320×568, 360×640, 390×844 and 430×932, in both themes, both HP panels sit on screen, the tray is fully visible and nothing overflows.
  - **Battle music:** the owner's 7 tracks are in `public/music/battle/`: Pip default-1, Wren battle-6, Captain Gale battle-4, Mara default-3, Old Hawkins battle-8, Professor Merlin elven, and wandering birders rotate through battle-2/8/6 and default-1. They play when Music is on in Settings, the previous music resumes afterwards, and the chiptune battle theme is the fallback.
- **Rival trainer portraits and RPG-style move names (v1.24, user request).**
  - **Portraits:** the owner's 60 trainer portraits are in `public/trainers/` (trimmed to a common frame, 128×192, quantised; 756KB total instead of 6MB). Cast: Pip t002, Wren t016, Captain Gale t023, Mara t024, Old Hawkins t020 (recast in v1.24.1 and again in v1.25 as the owner refreshed the set). Professor Merlin keeps his own sprite as the mascot. Wandering birders get one of the other 55, picked by the challenge's id.
  - **Where they appear:** the rival ladder (greyed when locked), the party screen, the battle intro card, the result quote, and the challenge pop-up (portrait vs your new bird).
  - **Rival speech:** whenever the rival speaks in battle (opening line, sending out their next bird, conceding or gloating), the dialogue box shows their name tag and portrait with the line in quotes. Narration stays plain.
  - **Move names:**
    - Quick: Echo Chirp, Glimmer Wing, Gale Wing, Quick Peck.
    - Super: Symphony Burst, Prismatic Dance, Tempest Dive, Harvest Frenzy.
    - Rival Big Moves are named per style (Thunder Chorus, Radiant Plume, Cyclone Dive, Feast Storm) and announced as "…is powering up Cyclone Dive! Guard!".
    - Signature skills (each still tied to its real-life fact): Daybreak Aria, Mirror Mimic, Prism Flash, Falcon Stoop, Crow's Gambit, Hidden Cache, Thunder Drum, Phantom Glide, Hyper Hover, Homing Comet, Aero Ace, Zen Stalk, Aqua Shield, Plunder Dive, Flock Rally.
- **Battle mode v1.25: growth, matchups, swapping and Trial Tickets (owner asked to enhance battle mode, and for birds to grow stronger and learn skills as players level up).**
  - **Birds learn moves as they level up** (birds level when they win):
    - Lv12: a style Technique fills the 4th move slot. Rally Anthem (Song) powers up for 3 turns; Mirage Veil (Display) makes the rival likely to miss and heals 15%; Tailwind Strike (Sky) never misses and adds 25% Super; Second Helping (Forage) heals 35%. Each recharges over 3 turns. Before Lv12 the slot shows locked with "Learns at Lv12", so players always see what's coming.
    - Lv20: the quick move upgrades (Sonic Trill, Dazzle Wing, Razor Gale, Fury Peck), ×1.3.
    - Lv30: the signature skill is mastered (name gets "+", 30% stronger; Zen Stalk ×2.6, Aqua Shield heals 35%).
    - Lv40: the Super becomes an Ultra move (Maestro Symphony, Aurora Showcase, Hypersonic Dive, Grand Harvest), ×1.2.
    - The party picker shows each bird's next move and level. The result screen announces what a bird learned when it levels up.
    - Rival birds follow the same rules, so later rivals use Techniques too.
  - **Trainer Bond:** +1% HP and power per trainer level after the first, capped at +30%. It's shown on the ladder, the party screen and the trainer level-up message, so leveling up as a player makes your birds stronger.
  - **Style matchups:** Song > Display > Forage > Sky > Song, each with a bird-true reason. A strong matchup is ×1.3, a weak one ×0.77, flagged as "Strong!/Weak…" on hits. Moves show ▲ Strong / ▼ Weak tags, and the party picker marks birds as ▲ Good pick or ▼ Risky against the rival's team. This adds real decisions to party order and swapping.
  - **Swap:** a Pokémon-style switch that uses your turn. Snack, Swap and Run moved to a row under the 2×2 move grid; the swap list shows HP and the matchup.
  - **Trial Tickets** (answers the owner's "users shouldn't battle whenever they please"):
    - Every rival battle or rematch costs one ticket.
    - Each logged bird earns one, capped at 5. Existing players start with 3.
    - The tutorial battle and wandering-birder challenges are free.
    - Shown on the ladder, the party button and the catch screen ("You earned a TRIAL TICKET!").
    - A "How it works" panel on the ladder explains tickets, matchups, growth and Bond.
- **Lenses (v1.25, owner request):**
  - The Legendary tier is removed. Owners are refunded each retired lens's price in Feather Coins on load and switched back to a lens they still own.
  - Rare lenses each have their own colour on the whole capsule body, with the hues spread out so no two look alike.
- **Battle mode v1.26: Pokémon / Final Fantasy battle flow (owner: battles felt unengaging, some taps didn't register, rival lines vanished too fast, intro portrait was cropped).**
  - **Lost taps fixed:**
    - Root cause: move buttons were a component declared inside the battle render, so every re-render rebuilt them. The 10-per-second turn timer made taps between press and release vanish.
    - Buttons are now plain render output, and the turn timer is removed (as in Pokémon; reading time matters more). FIGHT opens in about 65ms in testing.
  - **Command menu:** FIGHT / BAG / BIRDS / RUN.
    - FIGHT shows four moves with PP: quick move ∞; signature skill 5 PP; Technique 3 PP, or locked until Lv12; Guard ∞.
    - The header says who is faster and the style matchup.
    - The Super is a Final Fantasy-style **Limit** gauge above the menu.
  - **Turn order:** the faster bird acts first. Speed comes from flying ability plus level.
    - Guard, Bag, Birds and Run always go first, like Protect, items and switching.
    - A bird knocked out before it acts doesn't act.
    - Cooldowns are replaced by PP; out-of-PP or invalid moves don't use the turn.
  - **Readable text:**
    - Narration stays up for about 0.65–1.9s depending on length, and a tap skips it.
    - Rival speech waits for a tap (▼). This covers the opening line, sending out the next bird, and the win/lose quote.
    - "X uses Y!" now appears before the move animation.
    - A LOG button shows the whole battle's text, including everything the rival said.
  - **Status chips** under HP: PWR▲, PWR▼, FOCUS, SHIELD, DAZED.
  - **Intro:** "Rival Wren wants to battle!" with the full 2:3 portrait (no square crop).
